Challenges & Solutions

Challenges

1. Model Accuracy
Simplified models cannot fully reproduce complex hydrodynamic behaviour.
2. Forecast Uncertainty
Cyclone track and intensity can change with time.
3. Limited Validation
Prototype validation is based on selected historical events.
4. Data Gaps
Infrastructure and operational datasets may be incomplete.

Our Response

1. Historical Backtesting
Compare predictions against historical events and post-event SAR.
2. Ensemble Scenarios
Run multiple forecast scenarios and represent risk as a range.
3. Continuous Validation
Compare future predictions with observed post-event flooding.
4. Multi-source Integration
Combine multiple established datasets and build agency data-sharing pathways.
